# UX.md / User model

Who is on the other end. Established by research, not assumed.

**U1. They have done this roughly once in a decade.**
There is no learned pattern to draw on and nothing to remember from last time. Treat every person as a first-time user, because nearly all of them are.
*Constraint: guidance over efficiency. No shortcuts, no power-user affordances, no assumption that a convention will be recognized.*

**U2. They do not know insurance vocabulary.**
In testing, most people could not say what a deductible is. Many believed a claim would raise their rate regardless of fault. Terms are not just unfamiliar, they are actively misunderstood.
*Constraint: no term appears without a plain explanation next to it, the first time and every time.*

**U3. They are not coming back tomorrow.**
Nobody wants an account, a dashboard, or a saved preference. This is one errand on one bad day.
*Constraint: no registration before the report is filed. No settings. No features that pay off on a second visit.*

**U4. Ability varies widely, and skews older.**
The median claimant is 52. A meaningful share have reduced vision, tremor, or are operating one-handed with an injury.
*Constraint: large text and large targets. No timed interactions. No drag, no hover-dependent behavior, no small close buttons.*
