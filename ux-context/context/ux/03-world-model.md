# UX.md / World model

The conditions people are in while they use this. Not who they are, but where they are and what is happening around them.

**W1. Standing at the roadside, on a phone, within an hour of the crash.**
Two thirds of first reports arrive from a phone within sixty minutes. Often one-handed. Often outdoors in daylight glare. Sometimes in the rain.
*Constraint: design the phone first. Single column. High contrast. Nothing that needs two hands or a steady grip.*

**W2. Attention is poor and interruption is near certain.**
They are shaken, sometimes in pain. Police, a tow driver, or the other driver will interrupt. Testing showed people looking away from the screen every twenty to forty seconds.
*Constraint: one decision per screen. Every screen must survive being abandoned mid-answer and returned to cold.*

**W3. Documents are not at hand.**
Insurance card in the glovebox of a car that may be undrivable. Registration missing. Phone battery draining.
*Constraint: never block progress on a document. Anything from a document is optional or deferred.*

**W4. The connection is unreliable.**
Roadside, in a parking garage, under an overpass. Uploads fail. Pages half load.
*Constraint: save locally and sync. Tell people plainly when something has not been sent yet. Never lose an answer to a failed request.*

**W5. This is a legal record.**
The report is admissible. The submission time matters for coverage. People change their account of events once they are calm, and that is normal and expected.
*Constraint: timestamp the report visibly. Corrections must remain possible after submission, and the page must say so.*
