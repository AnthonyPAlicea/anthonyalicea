Report a crash

Tell us what happened and we will open your claim. Most people finish this in about ten minutes. Answer what you can, and give your best guess where you are not sure. We would rather have the report than have it perfect.

If it is an emergency, call 911

Call 911 first if anyone is hurt, the road is blocked, or you do not feel safe where you are. Come back and fill this out once everyone is okay.

Have these handy before you start

  • Your policy number, from your insurance card
  • Your license plate, and the other driver's plate if you got it
  • The other driver's name, phone, and insurance company
  • Photos of the damage and of the scene
  • The police report number, if police came

Fields marked optional can be left blank. We need everything else to open your claim.

What happens next

When you send this, you will see a "we got it" screen with your claim number, and a copy lands in your email.

Then a person takes over. The person handling your claim reads what you wrote, calls you at the number above, and walks you through getting the car looked at, what you pay toward the repair, and how we deal with the other driver's insurance. You do not have to chase them.

If anything changes before you hear from us, or you find the police report number, call the claims line at 1-800-555-0142.

By sending this report you are telling us that what you wrote is true as best you know it. If you remember something later, or got a detail wrong, call us and we will fix it.
