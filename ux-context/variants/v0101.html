Report a crash

Answer what you can and we will open your claim. You do not need an account and you do not need to know any insurance words. If you do not know an answer, leave it blank.

This is one page. Nothing is kept until you send it, so it is best to finish in one sitting. If you would rather stop and talk to a person, call 1-800-555-0142 and we will take your report over the phone.

If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911 first

This report can wait. Come back to it when everyone is safe and you are somewhere you can sit down.

What it helps to have nearby

None of it is required. It is easier if you gather it before you start.

  • Your insurance card, or a recent bill, for your policy number.
  • Whatever you wrote down or photographed at the scene, including the other driver's name, phone number, and insurance company.
  • The police report number, if an officer came and gave you one.
  • Photos of both cars and the place it happened, if you took any.

Photos

Photos let the person handling your claim see what you saw. The most useful ones are the damage up close, both cars from a few steps back, the license plates, and the road or parking lot around them. If you did not take any, or they are on another device, you can send them later. It will not hold up your claim.

9

Anything else

Weather, road work, a light that was out, another driver, or anything that did not fit above. If you are worried about something, put it here and the person handling your claim will read it.

What happens after you send this

  • You get a text and an email that says we got it, with a number for your report.
  • One person takes over from here. They will call you to go through what you sent and answer your questions. You can call them back directly at any point.
  • Before any repair work starts, they will tell you what you pay toward it. That amount is called your deductible, and it is set by your policy.
  • People often ask whether reporting a crash changes what they pay for insurance. Ask that person. They can look at your policy with you and give you a straight answer instead of a guess.
