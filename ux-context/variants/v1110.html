Report a car accident

This is the first report of an accident. It opens your claim, which is our record of what happened and the start of paying for the damage.

If anyone is hurt, stop and call 911

Dial 911 on your phone first. Get yourself somewhere safe, away from traffic. This report can wait as long as you need it to.

Before you start

What happens next

  1. You send this report. We record the date and time it reaches us, and that is the time that counts for your coverage.
  2. A claims specialist calls you at the number you give below. That is a person at Keystone who looks at what happened and sorts out what we pay. The call costs you nothing.
  3. You and the specialist sort out your car: towing it, where it gets repaired, and whether your policy covers a rental car while it is in the shop.
  4. You can add photos, fix a detail, or change your account of what happened after you send this. Nothing here is your last word on it.

You do not need your policy number, your insurance card, or a police report to send this. Every question below can be answered with "I am not sure," so you can finish now and fill in the rest later.
