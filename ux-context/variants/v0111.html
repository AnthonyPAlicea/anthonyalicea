Send your report

One person at Keystone takes care of your claim from here. They call you, they answer your questions, and they arrange the repair. You will hear from them within one business day.

How should the person handling your claim reach you?

Not sent yet. Pressing the button below is what sends it to us.

We stamp your report with the date and time it reaches us, and we show that time on the confirmation. Keep it. The time can matter for what your policy covers.

You can change anything in this report after you send it. Most people remember more once they have calmed down, and adding to your account later does not count against you. Call us and we will update it.

Sending this does not decide who was at fault, and it does not ask you for money. Before any repair starts, the person handling your claim will tell you plainly what you pay, if anything.
