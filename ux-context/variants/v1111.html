Report a crash

Tell us what happened to your car. Most people finish in about ten minutes. You do not need your policy number, your insurance card, or anything else from the glovebox.

What happens next

  1. 1

    You send this report and we got it. A confirmation with your claim number arrives by text or email within a few minutes.

  2. 2

    The person handling your claim calls you within one business day. It is the same person from that call onward, and you will have their direct number.

  3. 3

    They walk you through the repair and tell you what you pay toward it (your policy calls this your deductible) before any work starts. Nothing gets decided without you.

  4. 4

    If your car cannot be driven, we arrange the tow and a car for you to use. Call 1-800-555-0142 if you need a tow right now, and file this afterward.

Step 1 of 8

Photos of the crash

Photos tell us more than any description can, so start here while you are still at the scene. If it is safe, take them before the cars are moved.

Worth photographing

  • The damage to your car, close up
  • The damage to the other car
  • Both license plates
  • The whole scene from a few steps back, so we can see the street and where the cars ended up
  • Anything on the road that mattered, like ice, a pothole, or a hidden sign

Pick as many as you like, from your camera or from photos you already took. You can add more after you send the report.

Step 8 of 8

What happened, in your own words

This part is optional and you can send the report without it. If you do write something, write it the way you would say it to a friend. There is no right way to word it and this is not a test.

What you were doing, what the other car did, anything about the road or the weather that mattered.
