Car accident report

Report a car accident

You can fill this in from the roadside or from your kitchen table. It takes about ten minutes, and you do not need your policy number to start.

If anyone is hurt, call 911 first

This report can wait. When everyone is safe, come back and start with photos. You can also reach a person at Keystone at any hour on 1-800-555-0142.

What happens next

The whole process, before you fill in anything.

  1. You get a claim number right away

    A claim number is simply the file number for your accident. It appears on the next screen as soon as you send this, and we text and email you a copy.

  2. A claims specialist calls you within one business day

    That is the person at Keystone who takes your accident from here. They set up the repair shop, and a rental car if your policy pays for one. You can ask them anything, including questions you think are obvious.

  3. Someone looks at your car before any money is discussed

    Nobody expects you to know what the repairs cost. If your car cannot be driven, say so in step 4 and we will call you about a tow before anything else.

  4. You do not have to work out whose fault it was

    We do that later, from the photos, the police report, and both accounts of what happened. Nothing you write today settles it, and this form is not a legal statement.

1

Photos of the accident

Photos do more for your claim than anything you can write. If you are still at the scene, do this part now and leave the rest of the form until you are somewhere safe.

Useful shots, if you can take them safely:
  • All four corners of your car, including the parts that look fine
  • The other car, and its license plate
  • The whole scene from about ten steps back
  • A street sign, exit number, or storefront that shows where you are

Blurry photos are still useful. You can add more later using the link we text you.
