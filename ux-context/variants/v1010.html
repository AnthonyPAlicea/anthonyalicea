Auto claim

Report a car accident

Six short steps. Answer what you can. Every answer is saved on this device as you go, so you can stop, deal with what is in front of you, and come back to this page.

What happens next

  1. You get a claim number the moment you send this, on this page and by text message.
  2. A Keystone adjuster calls you within one business day, and sooner if your car cannot be driven.
  3. If you need a tow or a rental car, say so in step 4 and we start on it today.
  4. You can add photos, fix details, and change your account of what happened after you send this. People almost always do once they are calm, and it does not count against your claim.

Step 1 of 6

Photos of the scene

Start here. The scene changes fast, and photos are the single most useful thing you can give us. Words can wait until the end.

Photos stay on this device until the report is sent.

Shots that help, if you can get them safely

  • All four corners of your car, then close ups of the damage
  • The other vehicle, its damage, and its license plate
  • The street, the lane you were in, and any signs or signals
  • The whole scene from a few steps back
  • Anything on the road that mattered, such as ice, glass, or a pothole

No photos right now? Skip this. You can send the report without them and add photos to your claim later.

Send your report

This report becomes part of the legal record of the accident, and the time it arrives can matter for your coverage. Send it as soon as you can, even with blanks in it.

After you send it you can add photos, fill in blanks, and change your account of what happened. Corrections are expected and they do not weaken your claim.

Would rather talk to a person? Call 1-800-555-0142, any hour, any day. Have your name and phone number ready. You do not need your policy number.
