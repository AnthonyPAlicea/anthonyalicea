What happens next

  1. You send this and we got it. The screen shows the date and time your report reached us. That time is the one that counts for your coverage, so send it even if half of it is blank.
  2. The person handling your claim calls you within one business day. One person, on the number you give us below, with a direct line back to them.
  3. They sort out your car and your ride. Where your car goes, who looks at the damage, and whether your coverage includes a car to drive in the meantime.
  4. Before any repair starts, they tell you what you pay. The amount that comes out of your pocket, and the amount we cover. No work begins until you have heard both numbers.

Sending this report does not decide who was at fault and does not commit you to a repair. If you get a detail wrong, you can correct it after you send it. That is normal, and it does not put your claim at risk.
