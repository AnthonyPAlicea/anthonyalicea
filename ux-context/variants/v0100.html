Keystone

Report a car accident

This form opens a claim, which means asking Keystone to pay for repairs and other costs your policy covers. It takes about 10 minutes. There is no account to create and no password to remember.

Important

If anyone is hurt, or the cars are blocking traffic, stop and call 911 first. This report can wait. If you would rather tell a person what happened, call us at 1-800-555-0142, any hour, any day.

Before you start

It helps to have these nearby. Do not go looking for them now if you are still at the scene.

  • Your policy number. It is printed on your insurance card and at the top of your bill.
  • The date, the time, and the place the accident happened.
  • The other driver's name, phone number, and insurance company, if another car was involved.
  • The police report number, if an officer came to the scene.
  • Photos of the damage, if you took any on this device.

If you do not know an answer, leave it blank and keep going. A claims specialist, the person at Keystone who handles your repair from start to finish, will call you within one business day and fill in the rest with you.

Fields marked "Required" are the ones we cannot open a claim without. Everything else is optional.

6

Photos

The most useful shots are the damage on each car up close, both license plates, and one wide picture of the whole scene. A copy of the police report helps too. Photos are helpful but not required, and you can send more later by replying to the email we send you.
7

Anything else we should know

Other cars involved, damage to a fence or a mailbox, a dashcam recording, or anything that has been worrying you since it happened.

Send your report

What happens after you send this:

  1. The next screen shows your claim number. We also email it to you if you gave us an email address.
  2. A claims specialist calls you within one business day, at the phone number you gave in section 1.
  3. That person walks you through repairs, towing, and a rental car if your policy covers one.

Keystone Mutual claims line: 1-800-555-0142, any hour, any day. If anyone is in danger right now, call 911 instead.

Sending this report starts a claim. It does not change your policy, and nothing you enter here is saved until you press "Send my report."
