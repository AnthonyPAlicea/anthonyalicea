What happens next

  1. We got it. The moment you send this, we text you a report number so you have something to hold on to.

  4. We tell you what you pay, if anything, before any repair begins. No repair starts until you have that number.

1

Photos of the damage

We start here because it is the fastest part, and because photos tell us more than a paragraph can. Take them now if you are still at the scene.

Worth photographing, if you can get to them safely:

  • All four corners of your car, including the sides that look fine
  • The other car's license plate
  • The whole scene from about ten steps back
  • The road, the signals or signs, and anything on the ground
  • Your windshield, if it is cracked or starred

Up to 20 photos. Add more later if you think of something.
