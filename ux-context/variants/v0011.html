How we reach you

Best way to reach you

It is on your insurance card, which is probably in the glovebox. Do not go get it. Your name and phone are enough for us to find you.

What happens after you send this

  1. We got it. You will see it confirmed on this page and get a text with your report number.

  2. The person handling your claim calls you, usually within one business day, sooner if anyone was hurt.

  3. They walk you through what is covered, what happens to your car, and what you pay before your coverage starts.

  4. You can correct or add to this report at any point. People remember more once they are calm, and we expect that.

Send your report

We record the time you send this, and that time can matter for your coverage, so send it as soon as you have the basics. This report becomes part of your claim record. If you remember something differently later, tell us and we will add it. Changing your account of events is normal and it does not count against you.
