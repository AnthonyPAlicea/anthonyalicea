Report a car accident

Tell us what happened and we will open your claim. Most people finish in about ten minutes. You do not need your policy number.

If anyone is hurt or the road is blocked, call 911 first. This report can wait.

If you would rather tell a person, call the claims line at 1-800-555-0142, any hour of any day.

Before you start

What happens after you send this

  1. Right away

    You get a claim number on the screen as soon as you send the report, and again by text and email.

  2. Within one business day

    An adjuster calls you on the number you give below. They walk you through what your policy covers and answer the questions this page cannot.

  3. Your car

    If it cannot be driven, say so in step 4. We can arrange the tow and talk about a rental on that call.

  4. The rest of your day

    Nothing else is needed from you today. You do not have to decide anything about repairs or payment now.

Nothing here is final. If you do not know an answer, choose "I do not know" or leave it blank. You and your adjuster can correct or add anything later, and an honest gap will not cost you your claim.

What helps us most

  • All four corners of each vehicle, from a few steps back.
  • A close view of every dent, scrape, and broken light.
  • Both license plates, readable.
  • One wide shot that shows the road, the lane markings, and any signs.
