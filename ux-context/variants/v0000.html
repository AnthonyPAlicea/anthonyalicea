Auto claim

Report a car accident

Tell us what happened and we will open your claim. Everything is on this one page, and it takes most people about ten minutes. A claims specialist calls you within one business day of receiving this report.

If anyone is hurt or the scene is not safe, call 911 first. This form can wait.

Prefer to talk it through? Our claims line is open 24 hours a day at 1-800-555-0142.

What to have handy

  • Your Keystone policy number, from your ID card
  • Your driver license
  • The other driver's name, phone, and insurance details, if you were able to get them
  • The police report number, if officers came to the scene
  • Photos of the damage, the license plates, and the scene
